Pinacoteca Support

Pinacoteca is a board of small, calm widgets for your Mac: streaks, countdowns, goals and more.

Your data lives only on your Mac, in the app's own folder. To start fresh, use Settings and clear the dashboard.

Replay the welcome tour from Settings. Add a widget with New Widget, and use the search box to find any widget.

Questions, bugs or ideas: rana.flutterdev@gmail.com. We reply within a few days.
