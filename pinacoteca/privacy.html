Pinacoteca Privacy Policy

Pinacoteca keeps everything you create (widgets, notes, timers, progress) on your Mac. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

If you turn on Live currency rates in Settings, the currency widget requests today's exchange rates from open.er-api.com. That request contains no personal information. It is off unless you enable it.

Period and cycle dates you log in the Period Tracker are sensitive health-related data. They are stored only on your Mac, are never collected, shared, sold or used for advertising, and never leave your device. The tracker shows estimates from the dates you enter; it is not medical advice and not a contraceptive.

Picture folders you choose are read only on your Mac to show your own photos; they are never uploaded.
